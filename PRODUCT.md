# Local transcription desk

People helping with a transcript open their assigned local recording and draft TXT, listen, correct words and export an exact-basename TXT. The app has no backend, sign-in, uploads or shared online saving. IndexedDB retains text, language, playback preferences and content-version times on one browser. Audio is selected locally again after reload. Cross-tab version checks protect newer text from stale saves. The interface extends the established manuscript-first desk and keyboard behavior.
